Our Mission

The mission of this platform is to democratize access to Victorian road incident data, transforming complex, siloed records into an intuitive, data-driven experience. We aim to bridge the gap between raw transport data and actionable public insight, empowering users to move safely through their communities and advocate for infrastructure improvements. Supporting Our Users Our design philosophy prioritizes the specific goals and pain points of our target personas: Empowering the Regional Commuter: By enabling clear mapping and filtered analysis, we assist users like Marcus Vance, a regional driver in identifying safety trends on specific highways, helping him make informed decisions regarding his commute and vehicle safety needs. Facilitating Urban Advocacy: We provide the granular data necessary for users like Elena Rostova, an inner-suburban cyclist to cross-reference incident types with location and environmental conditions, thereby supporting her efforts to advocate for safer cycling infrastructure. Design & Heuristics To ensure the effectiveness and usability of this tool, our development adheres to core usability heuristics: Visibility of System Status: Users are kept informed about the nature of the data they are viewing through clear labeling and summary statistics. Match Between System and the Real World: We organize data in ways that reflect the realities of road travel and incident reporting, moving away from "bundled" data that confuses users like Marcus Vance. User Control and Freedom: We provide flexible filtering mechanisms that allow users like Elena Rostova to extract clean, concise datasets that meet their specific analytical requirements. Recognition Rather Than Recall: By summarizing facts across levels, we reduce the cognitive load on our users, allowing them to grasp the "big picture" of road safety before performing deep-dive analysis.
